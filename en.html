Physiotherapy, in English

We treat in German and English. If you have just moved here, are on a work assignment, or simply want to be sure you will be understood — this page has what you need. The booking calendar below is the real one.

What we do

Manual therapy

Hands-on treatment of joints and soft tissue for restricted movement, blockages and pain. 60 minutes, 92 €.

Physiotherapy / exercise therapy

Guided active rehabilitation, progressed session by session. 45 minutes, 57 €.

Sports physiotherapy

Return to load after injury: assessment, treatment and a graded plan back to your sport. 60 minutes, 98 €.

TMJ / jaw treatment (CMD)

For jaw clicking, teeth grinding, facial or tension headache. We work with your dentist. First appointment 60 minutes, 135 €.

Massage & lymphatic drainage

Classical massage from 54 €, manual lymphatic drainage after surgery from 42 €.

Athletic testing lab

Strength and jump testing with a written report, side-to-side comparison. 90 minutes, 215 €.

How payment works

  • We are a private practice. You receive an invoice from us and settle it directly.
  • Private health insurance and expat policies usually reimburse in full when you have a doctor's referral. Submit our invoice to your insurer.
  • Statutory insurance (gesetzliche Krankenkasse) cannot be billed by us. You are welcome as a self-payer — ask your doctor for a Privatrezept. In exchange you get a full 45 or 60 minutes, not the 15 to 20 the statutory system pays for.
  • No referral needed for massage, taping, sports physiotherapy or anything in the testing lab.
  • Cancellations are free up to 24 hours before. After that we charge half the fee.

Your first appointment

  • Bring your referral if you have one, your insurance details, and any imaging or medical reports.
  • Wear something you can move in. Towels are provided; changing rooms, lockers and showers are on site.
  • Arrive five minutes early — the time you book is when treatment starts.
  • Opening hours: Monday to Friday 08:00–19:00, closed 12:00–13:00. Saturday by arrangement.
